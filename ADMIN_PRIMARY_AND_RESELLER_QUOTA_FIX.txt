Admin and reseller quota update

1. Fixed adding secondary administrators in both mother and reseller bots.
   - Numeric Telegram IDs are compared consistently.
   - If the target user has no users-table row yet, one is created.
   - The operation reports failure instead of silently claiming success.

2. Added primary administrator management.
   - The administrators menu displays the current primary administrator.
   - The primary administrator can be changed by numeric Telegram ID.
   - Mother-bot changes persist in MAIN_ADMIN_USERID.
   - Reseller-bot changes update reseller_bots.admin_userid in the mother database.
   - Old and new primary administrators receive a transfer report.

3. Reseller-bot quota accounting for unlimited plans.
   - Every unlimited account consumes exactly 30 GB from the reseller bot quota.
   - Group orders consume 30 GB multiplied by account count.
   - Normal finite-volume plans continue to consume their actual configured volume.
   - User free-GB quota remains independent from the reseller bot's quota calculation.
